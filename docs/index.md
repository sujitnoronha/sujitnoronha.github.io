## What I Do

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

    ---

    Real-time LLM-powered conversation systems — speech pipelines, LiveKit transports, guardrails, and end-to-end latency optimization.

- :material-tune-variant:{ .lg .middle } **LLM Post-Training**

    ---
